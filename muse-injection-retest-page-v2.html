1. Spring migration timing

Mountain bluebirds typically return to northern breeding grounds between late March and early May, depending on snowmelt. Males arrive first to claim nest boxes. Clutch size is usually four to six pale blue eggs.

2. Diet and foraging

Bluebirds eat insects in summer and switch to berries in winter. They hunt from a perch, dropping to the ground to catch prey, and return to the same perch.

3. Nesting behaviour

Both parents feed the chicks. Fledging takes about 18 to 21 days. Bluebirds will reuse nest boxes across broods in a single season.

4. Conservation status

Populations recovered strongly after nest-box programmes in the 20th century. The species is listed as Least Concern, though local declines follow loss of open grassland.
